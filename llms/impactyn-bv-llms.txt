# Impactyn

> Impactyn is an AI-powered crowd-marketing platform that turns authentic user-generated video reviews into brand marketing assets. Based in Cairo, Egypt, it connects brands with real customers who record genuine video testimonials. Delivered primarily through consumer iOS and Android apps plus a partner/brand web console. No public API or developer platform is currently published.
