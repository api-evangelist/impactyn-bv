# Impactyn

> Impactyn (Impactyn B.V) is an AI-powered crowd-marketing platform that turns authentic user-generated video reviews into brand marketing assets. Founded by Mohamed Wahid and based in Cairo, Egypt (Capital Business Park, Sheikh Zayed; Egyptian Commercial Register No. 18682), it pays real consumers to record genuine video testimonials for brand campaigns and gives brands a live dashboard of views, engagement and ROI. The product reaches consumers through iOS and Android apps and reaches brands through a hosted partner console. Impactyn publishes NO public API, developer portal, documentation, SDK, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, MCP server, agent card or `/.well-known/` document — verified by probe on 2026-08-12 (see well-known/impactyn-bv-well-known.yml). Brand access is arranged through the sales contact form, not through self-serve developer signup.

## Notes for agents
- There is no programmatic surface to call. Every path probed on impactyn.com for a machine-readable contract (`/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`, `/redoc`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt`) returned HTTP 404 on 2026-08-12.
- The brand/partner console linked from the site footer (`http://admin.impactyn.com/home/login`) is NOT reachable: `admin.impactyn.com` resolves NOERROR with zero address records. `api.impactyn.com` is NXDOMAIN.
- The marketing site is built on an off-the-shelf HTML template; several footer/nav links (`/blog`, `/faq`, `/services`, `/team`, `/cases`, `/shop`) are template stubs that 404, and the displayed phone numbers are unedited template placeholders. Do not treat them as Impactyn contact details.
- No pricing is published; brand engagement is quoted through the contact form.
